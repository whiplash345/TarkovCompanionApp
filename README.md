# TarkovCompanionApp
Tarkov Companion App for Capstone final project at Full Sail University

## Introduction
This Tarkov companion app will serve one main feature, tracking progression for Tarkov players in Escape from Tarkov's new main story line. In Tarkov, tracking progressions towards certain endings can be difficult. There are 4 main endings, but in-game it is not clear what decisions lead to what ending. Different endings provide different rewards and often a player will want to reach a certain ending. This app will help users focus their time on working towards the specific ending they want, avoid unecessary missions, and not make any decisions that will lead them down a different path, away from the ending they want.

## Features

The progression feature will consist of three main frames:
- Main Task Progression
- Side Task Progression
- AI Chat Bot for assistence and answering questions

## Technologies and Development Setup

The app will be programmed in React Native and Type Script. I am developing the app primarily in VSCode while using an Android Studio emulator to view the progress. With the direction I am going, I may just have users store their progress on their local devices. The emulator is controlled entirely by expo. Specific steps for starting and finished a day's development are in the CodingHelp folder at the top of the root directory.

## Installation

There is currently no available instalation. This project is still in development and has not reached it's alpha yet.
